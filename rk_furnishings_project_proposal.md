# RK Furnishings Website Project Proposal

## 1. Project Title
**Development of a Premium Home Furnishings Website for RK Furnishings**

---

## 2. Introduction

RK Furnishings is a home furnishing showroom located in Bhimavaram, West Godavari District, Andhra Pradesh, India. The showroom provides premium interior furnishing solutions including curtains, blinds, sofa fabrics, wallpapers, carpets, mattresses, and flooring.

In today's digital world, having an online presence is essential for attracting customers and showcasing products. This project proposes the development of a **modern, responsive website** for RK Furnishings that will function as a **digital showroom and design consultation platform**.

The website will allow customers to browse product collections, view interior inspirations, and book home styling consultations.

---

## 3. Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Create a professional online presence for RK Furnishings
- Showcase furnishing products through an organized digital catalog
- Allow customers to explore home decor inspirations
- Provide an option for customers to book consultations
- Capture potential customer leads
- Improve business visibility through search engines

---

## 4. Scope of the Project

The website will include the following components:

- Product catalog displaying furnishing collections
- Dedicated service pages explaining styling and installation services
- Consultation booking system
- Interior design inspiration gallery
- Blog section for decor tips and trends
- Contact page with location map and WhatsApp integration

The platform will serve as both a **product showcase and a service platform** for customers looking to transform their homes.

---

## 5. Website Sitemap

### Home Page
The homepage will contain:

- Hero banner with interior design imagery
- Product category showcase
- Services offered by RK Furnishings
- Featured product collections
- Customer testimonials
- Contact and showroom information

---

### About Page
This page will describe:

- History of RK Furnishings
- Experience in the furnishing industry
- Mission and vision of the brand
- Commitment to quality and customer satisfaction

---

### Product Categories

The website will organize products into the following categories:

- Curtains & Drapes
- Sofa Fabrics
- Wallpapers
- Blinds
- Carpets & Rugs
- Mattresses
- Wooden Flooring

Each category page will display multiple product designs with images and descriptions.

---

### Services Page

RK Furnishings offers several services including:

- Home Styling Consultation
- Custom Curtain Stitching
- Window Measurement
- Installation Services
- Interior Styling Guidance
- NRI Home Setup Services

Customers will be able to book these services directly through the website.

---

### Inspiration Gallery

A visual gallery will showcase interior design ideas such as:

- Living room styling
- Bedroom curtain designs
- Modern wallpaper installations
- Elegant sofa fabric designs

This section helps customers visualize how products look in real homes.

---

### Blog Section

The blog will contain helpful articles related to interior design such as:

- Latest curtain trends
- Best sofa fabrics for Indian homes
- How to choose the right wallpaper
- Interior styling tips for small homes

This also helps improve search engine visibility.

---

### Contact Page

The contact page will include:

- Showroom address
- Phone number
- Email address
- Google Maps integration
- WhatsApp chat option

This makes it easy for customers to reach the business.

## 8. User Flow

Typical user interaction with the website will be:

1. User visits the homepage
2. Browses product categories
3. Views product details and inspirations
4. Books consultation or contacts RK Furnishings
5. RK Furnishings team responds and assists the customer
